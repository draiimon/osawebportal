📱 iOS / iPhone & iPad

iOS doesn't allow APK installs, but you can install the portal as a real app icon on your home screen — works the same way and runs full-screen.

  1. Open Safari (not Chrome) on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Go to https://eac.osawebportal.ct.ws
  3. Tap the Share button (square with arrow at the bottom).
  4. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  5. Tap Add. Done — the OSA Portal icon now appears on your home screen.
Admin users on iOS: after step 2, navigate to /admin/ first, then perform the Add to Home Screen step. The icon will open the admin login directly.

🤖 Android — How to install the APK

  1. Tap Download for Android above.
  2. Open the downloaded .apk file from your Notifications or Files app.
  3. If Android prompts "Install unknown apps" — tap Settings → enable for your browser → go back.
  4. Tap Install. Done — the OSA Portal app icon will appear in your launcher.
Android may show a Play Protect warning the first time. This is normal for apps installed outside the Play Store. Tap Install anyway to continue.
