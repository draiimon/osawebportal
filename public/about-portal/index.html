About Emilio Aguinaldo College

Emilio Aguinaldo College

Mission, vision, and philosophy are stated as published on the College website.

Mission

Emilio Aguinaldo College provides a learner-centered, inquiry-based, and socially relevant academic community.

Vision

Emilio Aguinaldo College envisions itself as an internationally recognized private non-sectarian academic institution rooted in the Filipino nationalist tradition that consistently pursues the advancement and welfare of humanity.

Philosophy

Emilio Aguinaldo College is a private, non-sectarian, co-educational institution of learning that fosters equal and fair opportunities of education, the total development of our students, conscious of their national identity and their role in the global community.

Core values

As stated on the institutional site: Virtue, Excellence, and Service.

OSA Cavite Campus

Official Office of Student Affairs Summary (Cavite-Focused)

The Office of Student Affairs (OSA) is an academic-support unit responsible for programs and services that develop the non-academic aspects of student life. Each school year, OSA conducts need-based and student-focused activities designed to strengthen Emilian values and life skills in alignment with institutional goals.

Duties and functions
  • Acts as the link between EAC administration and the student body.
  • Creates opportunities for active student participation in college affairs.
  • Oversees student councils and organizations with guidance on leadership and development.
  • Coordinates with recognized organizations to ensure alignment with school rules and policies.
Core services offered

OSA administers a wide range of services that support student welfare, discipline, guidance, and co-curricular development across the Cavite campus.

  • Student Orientation
  • Scholarship Applications and Inquiries
  • Accreditation of Student Councils and Student Organizations
  • Student Conduct and Discipline
  • Student Manual Development and Review
  • Issuance of Clearance and Good Moral Character Certificate
  • Lost and Found Item Documentation
