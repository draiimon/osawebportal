OSA Services

These entries follow the Office of Student Affairs role in student life and development at EAC.

Student Conduct and Discipline

OSA attends to student conduct and discipline in line with college policies—consistent with the OSA mandate on student life and welfare.

Lost & Found

Coordinates logging and release of found items reported on campus through OSA, so students have a single office to report or claim property.

ID Processing

School ID services for new students, replacement, and updates are processed through OSA following campus procedures and posted requirements.

Scholarship Applications and Inquiries

Listed under official OSA services: assistance with scholarship applications and inquiries, including requirements and schedules published by the institution.

Issuance of Good Moral Character Certificate

OSA processes requests for Good Moral Character certificates (and related clearance documentation) according to published procedures and release schedules.

ISO and PACUCOA Accreditation

Student-affairs support tied to institutional quality assurance and accreditation (e.g., ISO, PACUCOA) is coordinated through OSA as the liaison for student-facing requirements.

Learner Activity Program (LAAP)

OSA supports co-curricular and student activities under LAAP—aligning programs with institutional guidelines and student organization rules.
